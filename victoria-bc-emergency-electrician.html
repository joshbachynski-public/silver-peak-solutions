Pro-Tip: Before agreeing to any emergency electrical service, always request a detailed, itemized estimate that breaks down labor, materials, service call fees, and any potential surcharges for after-hours or weekend work. Ask the electrician to clarify what is included and what could trigger additional costs, such as diagnostic fees or unexpected repairs. Insist on written documentation and avoid verbal agreements, as this protects you from hidden fees and overcharges. If possible, compare estimates from multiple providers—even in urgent situations—to ensure pricing is fair and transparent.
